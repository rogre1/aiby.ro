Edit your reels with one message

This is the message I send Claude Code when I have a raw video. It cuts the pauses and the "umm"s, adds captions word by word and exports a reel that's ready for Instagram. You just watch it and post.

⏱ 5 minutesNo editing appFree prompt

Before you start

  1. Have Claude Code on your computerClaude Code works with a paid Claude plan. Never used it? My free beginner guide sets it up step by step, with a screen for every click.
  2. Put your video in one folderFor example a folder called "reels" on your Desktop. A video of you talking works best.
  3. Open that folder in Claude CodeThen paste the message below and press Enter.

Copy this
I'm not technical, so explain every step in one short sentence.
If you need to install a tool, tell me what it is and ask me first.

In this folder there's a video called [reel.mov]. Turn it into a reel:
1. Write down everything I say, word by word.
2. Cut the silences and filler words like "umm". Keep it natural and never cut in the middle of a word.
3. Add captions 2 to 3 words at a time, in [my font], white, with the most important word in [my brand color]. Put them in the lower third, never over my face.
4. Make it 9:16 (1080x1920) and a little warmer.
5. Save it as reel_final.mp4 next to the original and tell me where it is.

At the end, show me a short list of what you cut, so I can ask you to put anything back.

Fill in the boxes above and the prompt updates itself. Or copy it as is and fill in the [brackets] in Claude Code.

What you'll get back

What Claude Code tells you (shortened)
✓ I need a free video tool called ffmpeg. Can I install it? → you: yes
✓ Wrote down all 142 words
✓ Cut 11 pauses and 4 "umm"s (the video went from 48 to 36 seconds)
✓ Added captions in your font and color
✓ Saved reel_final.mp4 in your reels folder
Tip: Not happy with something? Just write it like you'd tell a person: "the captions are too small" or "put back the part where I laugh". The first video takes a few extra minutes for setup. After that it's fast.
