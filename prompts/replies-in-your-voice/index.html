Customer replies that sound like you

When you ask AI to answer a customer, it writes like a call center: "Dear valued customer, thank you for reaching out." This prompt shows it 3 messages you wrote yourself, so it learns how you talk and answers the way you would.

⏱ 3 minutesWorks in ChatGPT or ClaudeFree

Before you start

  1. Open ChatGPT or ClaudeThe free version is fine. A new, empty chat.
  2. Copy 3 messages you sent to customersFrom WhatsApp, Instagram or email. Normal everyday replies are perfect. Remove anything private, like phone numbers.
  3. Have one new customer message readyThe one you need to answer right now.

Your Voice Guide
  1. Start with "Hi" and their first name. Never "Dear".
  2. Short: 2 to 3 sentences.
  3. Warm and direct. Answer the question first, then the details.
  4. One emoji at most, usually 😊 at the end.
  5. ... and 6 more rules
The reply, in your voice

Hi Noa! Yes, I do gluten free 🙂 For 20 people it's $85, and I can have it ready Friday afternoon. Want me to save the date for you?

Tip: Save your Voice Guide. Next time, paste it with any new message (or add it to ChatGPT's custom instructions) and skip the first part.
